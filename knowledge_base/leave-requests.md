# Leave requests

Annual leave, sick leave, and unpaid days are handled by HR. Time off is
booked in the HR leave tool, not an IT ticket. The helpdesk cannot approve
leave or add days to a payroll calendar.

Submit before the first missed workday. Your manager sees the request in
that tool and can approve or ask you to pick different dates. If you were
already out unexpectedly, still record the absence in the same place so the
calendar matches reality — but do not make late booking a habit.

Public holidays are already marked. Do not file a separate request for those
dates, and do not email IT asking someone to "block" a holiday. If plans
change, cancel or edit the booking in the HR leave tool. A cancellation that
only lives in chat will not reach payroll.

1. Open the HR leave tool and choose the leave type.
2. Enter the dates and submit before the first missed workday.
3. Watch that tool for manager approval or a request to change dates.

Use this when you will be away from work. Do not use it for equipment or
account problems.
