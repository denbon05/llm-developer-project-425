# VPN access

Use the company VPN when you need internal file shares, printers, or other
private hostnames while you are away from the office network. Public websites
do not require the tunnel. This page covers how to get a working client and
what not to install.

Personal laptops enroll through the intranet VPN page. Open that page in a
browser on the laptop you will take home, run the listed installer for your
operating system, and sign in with your usual work account when the client
prompts you. Split tunnel is default: only company destinations go through
the tunnel, which keeps ordinary web traffic off the corporate path.

Do not install a second client on a company laptop. Managed machines already
carry the approved client. A second product often breaks routing and is not
supported. Do not download a VPN installer from a search result or a
forwarded chat file.

When you are already on the office LAN, disconnect the VPN. At home, a hotel,
or another untrusted network, connect before you open internal file shares.
If the client will not connect, go back to the intranet VPN page and confirm
you used that installer rather than an unofficial package.

1. On the laptop you will use remotely, open the intranet VPN page.
2. Install the listed client and complete sign-in.
3. Connect, then open an internal file share to confirm the tunnel works.

Disconnect when you return to the office so local printers and wired access
are not forced through the tunnel.
