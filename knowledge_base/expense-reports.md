# Expense reports

Work travel and small out-of-pocket purchases are reimbursed through
finance, not through IT. Finance claims need a photo of the receipt
within 10 calendar days. A card statement alone is not enough.

Photograph the receipt so the merchant name, date, and amount are readable.
If the paper copy is lost, you still need an image of a merchant reissue or
equivalent proof. Meals with no receipt are not reimbursed. Mileage uses
the same claims path; include the trip dates.

1. Take a clear photo of the receipt.
2. Send the claim to finance within 10 calendar days of the purchase.
3. Keep the original until the payment lands.

Do not attach only a monthly card statement. Do not ask IT to store receipts
on a ticket. Late claims after the 10 calendar days window are often
refused, so do not wait for a batch of trips to pile up.

If finance asks for a clearer image, reply with a new photo of the same
receipt rather than a screenshot of your banking app.
