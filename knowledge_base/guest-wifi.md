# Guest Wi-Fi

Visitors, contractors, and other short-term guests must not join the
employee wireless network. Guests use a reception day-pass. That is the
only supported way to get them online in the building.

Walk the guest to reception and ask staff to issue a kiosk PIN. The PIN
expires at midnight local time and is not reused the next morning. Read it
to the guest; do not leave it on a shared notepad overnight.

Never share the employee Wi-Fi password. Sharing it is a policy violation,
and guests often lose access when the staff password rotates. A reception
day-pass is only for guest wireless in this building. It does not grant
internal file shares or a work account.

1. Take the guest to reception rather than sending them to wander the floors.
2. Have reception issue a day-pass PIN from the kiosk.
3. Stay until the guest can load a public website.

If reception is unstaffed, wait for staff to return. Do not offer your own
password as a workaround.
