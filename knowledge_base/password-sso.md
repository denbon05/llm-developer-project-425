# Password and SSO

Work accounts use single sign-on. The same sign-in covers email and most
internal apps. IT does not keep a spare copy of your password and cannot set
a new one from a helpdesk ticket.

Password reset is only the identity portal self-service. Open the portal from
a browser where you are signed out, start the forgotten-password flow, and
finish every prompt on screen. Never email a password — not to IT, not to a
manager, and not to yourself "for safekeeping."

If the portal reports a lockout, wait 15 minutes and try the same
self-service flow again. Extra guesses during that window extend the wait.
Do not ask a colleague to reset the account for you, and do not paste a new
password into chat.

After a successful reset, sign in once on a device you trust and confirm
email opens. If you still cannot sign in after the wait, stay in the
identity portal and use the remaining recovery options there.

1. Sign out and open the identity portal.
2. Run the self-service reset to the end.
3. On lockout, wait 15 minutes and retry. Never send the password by email.
