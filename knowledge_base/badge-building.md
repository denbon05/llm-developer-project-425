# Badge and building access

The office uses a proximity badge. Do not hold a secured door open for
someone you cannot identify. During ordinary daytime hours, entry is
badge-only at the main doors.

After 19:00 the lobby is closed. Use the side-entrance intercom and quote
your desk number. Security will not accept a name shouted through the glass,
and they will not buzz you in from a hallway phone. Daytime is badge-only;
do not use the after-hours path while the lobby is still open.

A lost badge is replaced the same day at the lobby desk during staffed
hours. Temporary paper passes are not issued after hours. If you forgot
your badge, wait for staffed hours or retrieve it; do not ask a visitor to
let you through.

1. During the day, badge in at the main entrance.
2. After 19:00, go to the side entrance, use the intercom, and quote your
   desk number.
3. For a lost badge, use the lobby desk while it is staffed.

Do not prop a door with a bag. Do not share your badge. After-hours entry
is only the side-entrance intercom path described above.
